# JEV-like bounded-decision public visual brief

## Purpose

Produce two complementary public-beta views from the frozen JEV-like capability case:

1. a self-contained interactive HTML that explains the observed capability, the unclosed adoption bridge, and two conditional structural outcomes;
2. a Markdown-native cover graphic that remains legible inside the repository README.

This is a technology-shock discovery and structural-forecast demo. It is not a claim that a new industry waist or a company winner already exists.

## Memory sentence

Typed bounded decisions may make repetitive semantic judgment cheaper and easier to embed in software, but durable control is more likely to sit with whoever defines the question and relevant state, calibrates confidence, accepts outcomes, and owns exceptions and feedback.

## Frozen analytical boundary

- Qualified output `Y`: a bounded semantic judgment that is safely consumed by software as a typed choice, score, probability, or correct escalation under the workflow's quality and liability threshold.
- Transferred function `Q`: evaluate a finite choice, score, or yes/no probability from text or structured state and return a typed result directly consumable by software.
- Scope `Omega`: JEV-like typed decision models and adjacent lightweight routers, safety classifiers, and rerankers when they replace the same bounded-decision function. Exclude open writing, candidate invention, complex planning, exact arithmetic, and final legal or human authorization.
- Base-case evidence cutoff: 2026-10-29. Public capability-discovery update: 2026-11-05.
- Event anchor: TypeSafe AI's JEV interfaces. The research object is the broader functional class, not one vendor.
- Current state: a recognizable capability class and multiple adjacent implementations exist; cross-industry production task share, stable calibration, long-term unit economics, and durable single-vendor rent are not established.
- The visual must not turn product/interface evidence into adoption, industry structure, or owner capture.

## Primary current path

Keep the first view to these decision-changing functions:

```text
business state
-> question + answer-space definition
-> relevant-state preparation and deterministic calculation
-> typed bounded-decision model [capability widened / backend may be replaceable]
-> confidence and acceptance gate
   -> automatic low-risk action
   -> generator/planner for open tasks
   -> accountable human handling for high-risk or uncertain cases
-> outcome labels and exception results
-> feedback to questions, state selection, and thresholds
```

The primary present-tense message is that the capability exists but the adoption bridge is not closed. Do not draw a formed industry cone in the default state.

## Three required states

### 1. Observed capability — default

- Show a typed bounded-decision interface and multiple adjacent implementation paths.
- Keep production adoption, calibration quality, task share, and vendor rent conditional or unknown.
- The visible constraint is the adoption bridge: domain state, question design, threshold calibration, exception ownership, and acceptance rights.

### 2. Conditional diffusion — adoption bridge closes

- Repetitive classification, routing, scoring, and guardrail judgments move out of general generation where quality and liability thresholds are met.
- The general-LLM parsing/retry middle layer relaxes.
- The successor cut moves toward definition, relevant state, labels, calibration, observability, acceptance, and exception operations.
- More atomic decisions may be embedded in software, but this is a conditional demand-expansion path.
- Vertical workflow owners or runtimes may gain control only when they hold deployment, modification, acceptance, and reusable feedback rights.

### 3. Absorbed or bypassed — separate category does not form

- Small general-purpose models with structured output, deterministic rules, or conventional classifiers remain good enough.
- Decomposition maintenance, sparse labels, latency, or liability prevent broad migration.
- Cloud or frontier-model vendors absorb the interface, or workflows return to one general model/backend.
- The specialized class remains a feature or local tool rather than an independent waist.

## Strongest counterforce

Small general-purpose models, structured-output APIs, deterministic rules, and conventional classifiers may erase the need for a separate bounded-decision category. Show this as a real bypass, not a footnote.

## Largest unknown

There is no established cross-industry measure of production task share, stable calibration under drift, end-to-end total cost, or liability-adjusted acceptance for this class. Outcome labels may be too sparse or delayed to close the learning loop.

## Feedback-loop contract

The visible return path must name:

```text
production outcome or exception
-> label / failure signal
-> workflow owner with modification rights
-> changed state selection, question, or threshold
-> acceptance gate in the next decision cycle
```

Do not imply that outside observers or model vendors automatically receive this feedback. The loop may remain private inside a vertical workflow owner.

## Four-direction event labels

- Substitution / relaxation: less general-LLM token use, parsing, retry, and some manual first-pass work for bounded decisions.
- Complement activation: state engineering, evaluation, calibration, observability, and exception operations become more valuable.
- Interface convergence: a shared typed decision contract could make hosted, open, or cloud-integrated backends more substitutable.
- Demand expansion / pruning: more micro-decisions may enter software; open-ended, exact, or high-liability work remains outside the class.

## Owner-capture boundary

- Vertical SaaS or enterprise workflow owner: conditional control through definitions, state, permissions, acceptance, labels, and feedback.
- Runtime or model gateway: conditional control through routing and evaluation, limited by replaceable backends or cloud absorption.
- Specialized model provider: possible high-volume revenue, limited by switching and open alternatives.
- Human review / BPO: work may shift toward uncertain, high-loss, accountable exceptions; total employment is unknown.

Structural position must never be labeled as proven profit or durable rent.

## Visual hierarchy

- First glance: the typed capability widened; the adoption bridge is still the unresolved passage.
- Second glance: if diffusion occurs, the constraint migrates to definition, state, calibration, acceptance, exceptions, and feedback rights.
- Third glance: a strong bypass can absorb the category into general models, rules, classifiers, or cloud control planes.
- Use topology, branching, gate shapes, a visible private feedback return, and a clear observed-versus-conditional distinction.
- Do not make a dashboard of equal-weight cards the primary visual.
- Do not repeat conditional prose across every node; place details in the state switcher and drill-down.
