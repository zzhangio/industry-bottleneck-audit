# Trial feedback

Bring one real question that is not one of the public examples. Run the skill without maintainer coaching and keep the original prompt and answer.

```text
Trial ID:
Skill version or commit:
Model and reasoning level:
Question and decision being made:
Evidence cutoff and search access:
Quick Scan or Full Audit:
Elapsed time:

1. Could you state the accepted output and case boundary after reading the result?
2. Which important node, edge, bypass, or acceptance right was missing?
3. Did the result separate industry dependence from one company's capture?
4. Did local versus parent learning loops change your interpretation?
5. Which claim looked precise but lacked adequate evidence?
6. What was the strongest useful insight?
7. What action, search, or decision changed because of the result?
8. What observable result would falsify the main conclusion?
9. Was this a suitable use case for the skill? Why or why not?
10. May an anonymized version be retained as an evaluation case?
```

Do not post confidential documents or personal data in a public issue. A useful negative result may be `no stable bottleneck`, `insufficient evidence`, or `unsuitable case`.
