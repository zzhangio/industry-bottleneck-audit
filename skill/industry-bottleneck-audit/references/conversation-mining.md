# Conversation Sources

Use conversation sources only when the user authorizes recovering or consolidating prior research. Read only relevant, accessible material.

- Record a stable source reference, date or turn locator, and whether the original was checked. If unavailable, label the limitation; do not invent quotations or an ex-ante timestamp.
- Treat dialogue as provenance for a question or hypothesis, not external evidence for an industry fact. Repeated statements and agent summaries are not independent confirmations.
- Check factual claims against traceable sources under the ordinary evidence protocol. Keep facts, inference, and unknowns distinct.
- Preserve dated prior claims when making an update; use the hypothesis and incremental-update protocol in [method.md](method.md). A later reconstruction is not an earlier prediction.
- Do not publish raw conversations, unrelated personal content, credentials, or local paths. Share only relevant derived material within the user's authorized scope.

The output may be a short source-backed question or hypothesis with its uncertainty and next evidence requirement. This source-handling module does not establish empirical validation; use [evaluation.md](evaluation.md) for testing the skill.
