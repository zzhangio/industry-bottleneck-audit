# Human Review Compression

Use this module for outputs a person must inspect, challenge, compare, or remember. Its purpose is editorial compression, not analytical simplification. The full graph, claim ledger, unknowns, and alternative paths remain available in the machine artifact or drill-down.

## Compression Gate

Before presenting the result, freeze the decision question and the qualified output `Y`. If materially different products, geographies, qualification regimes, or system outputs have different acceptance paths, give them separate selectable views or separate figures. Do not force them into one universal minimum cut.

The primary human view should normally contain only the decision-changing path:

```text
accepted output
-> current serial or sparse qualified path
-> candidate cut or acceptance gate
-> strongest bypass
-> likely successor constraint
-> largest unknown or falsifier
```

Keep the view small enough to scan, normally about five to nine decision-changing nodes. Preserve the full topology behind a drill-down, appendix, or machine artifact. More nodes are justified only when removing one would hide a distinct substitution, qualification, or rights path.

## Node And Edge Discipline

- Use one abstraction level in the primary path. Do not place a broad sector such as `materials` beside a specific subsystem such as `EUV optics` as if they were equivalent nodes.
- Name nodes as human-recognizable functions, departments, interfaces, or acceptance gates. Internal scores and model intermediate variables belong in details, not as the primary labels.
- A visible node must be independently replaceable, acceptable/rejectable, expandable, or falsifiable. Merge descriptive categories that do not change the path.
- Label important edges with the transmitted object: qualified wafer, model boundary, process window, measurement confidence, package bandwidth, liability, or another concrete quantity.
- Show company or department names only where the mapping is supportable. Keep owner capture distinct from system importance.
- Use topology, branching, channel width, or gate shapes when the point is bottleneck structure. A collection of cards or a wide comparison table is not a substitute for a functional map.

## Static First, Conditions Second

The first view shows the current observed or best-supported static structure at the declared cutoff. Do not fill the primary map with repeated `if`, `if not`, failure-condition, and alternative-future prose.

Move conditional material into one of these secondary surfaces:

- node drill-down for bypass, release condition, evidence, and unknowns;
- a scenario control that changes only the affected nodes or edges;
- a separate technology-shock layer;
- an appendix or claim table for full falsifiers and counterevidence.

Do not hide a decisive uncertainty. Keep the single largest unknown and evidence cutoff visible in the first view, while retaining the complete list in details.

## Human And Machine Parity

The human view is a projection over the canonical evidence, not a rewritten summary. Stable node, edge, and claim IDs should link the compressed view back to the full bundle whenever the workflow maintains one. Visual emphasis may change; factual state, epistemic status, owner mapping, falsifiers, and observed-versus-forecast boundaries may not.

Machine-to-machine outputs may remain high-dimensional and need not satisfy the small-node or first-viewport constraints. They must still preserve case boundaries and stable identifiers so a later human view can be generated without reinterpreting the case.

## Review Test

A human-facing artifact passes only when a reviewer can answer, from the first view and one level of drill-down:

1. What qualified output is this map about?
2. Which few functions currently narrow or accept it?
3. What is the strongest viable bypass?
4. Where does the constraint move if the current cut widens?
5. Who controls acceptance or feedback, and does that imply owner capture?
6. What is the largest unknown and what observation would change the picture?

If the reviewer must read a long report before answering these questions, the analysis may be complete but the human rendering has failed. Compress the presentation rather than deleting evidence.

## Career And Company Overlays

Do not mix career suitability or named-company attractiveness into the first structural pass. First locate the functional cut. Then let the reviewer toggle or click an overlay showing:

- responsible departments and example companies;
- the rights available to a role: validation, model, process-window, modification, customer-problem, or feedback rights;
- entry distance and skill bridge;
- company capture conditions and counterpower.

This prevents personal fit or company fame from replacing the industry judgment.
