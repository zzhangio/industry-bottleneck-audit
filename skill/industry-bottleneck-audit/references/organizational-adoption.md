# Organizational Adoption And Jurisdiction Protocol

Use this module when AI, automation, or another capability may change jobs, management layers, firm boundaries, monitoring, contracting, or task jurisdiction. It converts organizational scenarios into auditable structural hypotheses. It was informed by Hemanth Asirvatham and Elliott Mokski's 2026 OpenAI-hosted essay, *The future of the firm*, but does not treat that essay's scenarios as observed outcomes or OpenAI institutional claims.

## 1. Freeze The Organizational Output

Do not analyze “the firm” in the abstract. Freeze:

```text
Y = coherent, accepted organizational output
Q = local work and context transformed into coordinated decision and action
Omega = function, firm type, geography, responsibility regime, workforce, and date
```

Name the current human or organizational carrier of `Q`. Separate formal authority from practical influence.

## 2. Locate Capability And Adoption

Record capability scope separately from organizational position.

### Capability scope

```text
task -> job -> team -> function -> firm
```

Moving right requires broader competence, context handling, exception coverage, and responsibility. It may reduce the number of human interfaces required for adoption, but usually increases qualification and failure burden.

### Adoption position

- `beneath`: AI is delegated execution under an existing worker. Capability moves; formal task jurisdiction and final acceptance mostly remain human.
- `beside`: AI receives a bounded role or team remit alongside people. Some delegation and modification rights move; acceptance and responsibility may remain human.
- `between`: AI aggregates information, coordinates, monitors, or recommends between organizational layers. Observability and agenda-setting may centralize even without formal managerial authority.
- `outside`: the capability is purchased through a contractor, platform, or service and may lower market transaction costs rather than internal coordination costs.
- `mixed`: more than one position is materially active; decompose when their rights or scale effects differ.

Name the initiating actor. A frontline worker, manager, executive, customer, regulator, or vendor faces different context and retraining costs.

## 3. Capability Is Not Jurisdiction

For each path, distinguish:

```text
can perform
-> can access context
-> may be delegated the task
-> may modify the workflow
-> may accept or reject output
-> may allocate resources
-> may be challenged or overridden
-> bears responsibility
```

An AI system can occupy an execution niche without owning task jurisdiction. Do not describe influence, recommendation, or default routing as formal authority unless the evidence supports it.

Map at least these rights when material:

- value definition;
- constraint encoding;
- delegation and resource allocation;
- acceptance;
- modification;
- appeal and override;
- responsibility;
- feedback ownership.

## 4. Firm-Boundary Differential

AI may lower both internal coordination cost and external market-contracting cost. The sign of firm-size change is therefore conditional:

```text
internal coordination cost falls faster
+ common assets, mission, liability, or feedback remain important
-> more internalization or a larger holding structure becomes plausible

external contracting, monitoring, and interface cost falls faster
+ assets are modular and responsibility can be specified
-> outsourcing, contractor networks, or more small firms become plausible
```

Record common assets, asset specificity, incomplete-contract exposure, data access, brand coherence, liability, and feedback ownership. “AI reduces coordination cost” alone cannot determine whether firms widen or shrink.

## 5. Likely Constraint Migration

Common transitions include:

```text
manual execution widens
-> problem definition and constraint encoding tighten
-> organizational context access tightens
-> exception handling and acceptance tighten
-> responsibility, appeal, and expertise reproduction tighten
```

Entry-level task removal can create a delayed expertise-reproduction Gate even when current output improves. Register the lag and observable rather than asserting inevitable deskilling.

For monitoring and management systems, test both directions:

- **decentralizing path:** better exception detection lets workers act with broader local discretion;
- **centralizing path:** pervasive legibility lets executives enforce one objective and bypass intermediary interpretation.

The deciding evidence is rights allocation, intervention frequency, override and appeal outcomes, not model capability alone.

## 6. Required Scale Balance

Never infer employment contraction from task automation. Reconcile:

- qualified output volume;
- labor per unit;
- total labor or headcount;
- role, wage, and authority mix;
- number and size distribution of firms or organizational units;
- internal versus external labor;
- unit cost and total spend;
- where released capacity is absorbed;
- demand rebound;
- training and apprenticeship pipeline.

Use `contraction`, `productivity_release`, `scale_absorption`, `downstream_expansion`, `role_recomposition`, `externalization`, or `unresolved` only after stating the denominator.

## 7. Falsifiable Output

For each material adoption path, record:

| Field | Required question |
|---|---|
| `capability_scope` | What complete task bundle can be delivered at the acceptance threshold? |
| `adoption_position` | Beneath, beside, between, outside, mixed, or unknown? |
| `initiating_actor` | Who can insert the capability with the least organizational learning? |
| `required_context` | Which data, tacit knowledge, permissions, interfaces, and physical access are required? |
| `delegated_rights` | Which rights actually move? |
| `retained_rights` | Which human or institutional rights remain decisive? |
| `jurisdiction_shift` | None, partial, substantial, or unknown? |
| `integration_burden` | Who must retrain, reconfigure, supervise, or accept liability? |
| `observable` | Which workflow, contract, intervention, override, headcount, or firm-boundary evidence would reveal the shift? |
| `falsifier` | What would show capability improved without organizational migration? |

Treat scenario essays, demos, and product releases as hypothesis inputs. They do not establish adoption, rights migration, scale effects, or retained rent without corresponding evidence.
