# Quick Scan Trial Contract

Use this route for a first-pass industry, company, technology, or event question. It is a bounded decision artifact, not a compressed full audit.

## 1. Intent Gate

Before research, infer and freeze:

```text
Audience decision: what should the user be able to decide after this scan?
Decision question: what structural claim is being tested?
Scope: the provisional Y/Q/Omega/t boundary.
Hypothesis under test: optional and explicitly provisional.
Non-goals: what the scan will not claim.
```

Do not turn this into a questionnaire. Ask only when an unresolved boundary would materially change the answer. After the evidence and objection check, write or replace the final memory sentence and claim ceiling; never freeze the answer before research.

## 2. Canonical Journey

Show one main journey:

```text
real demand
-> function that must be performed
-> candidate narrowing or acceptance gate
-> qualified delivered output
-> payment, control, or feedback capture
```

Add one substitute, bypass, or counterforce. If a technology or event is the starting point, show:

```text
capability delta
-> directly relaxed task
-> released quantity
-> next constraint
-> possible rights or rent movement
```

Keep alternate architectures out of the main journey unless one overturns the conclusion.

When the user's question is specifically why the local improvement has or has not become an industry change, add only the shortest local loop, the parent loop, and their bridge condition. Do not force the full ordinal profile into a quick scan unless the comparison depends on it; use [closed-loop-propagation.md](closed-loop-propagation.md) for a full or comparative reconstruction.

For a technology or product claim, add one compact shock check before tracing propagation:

```text
status: demonstrated [F] or stipulated scenario [H]
comparison baseline and acceptable-output threshold
deployment maturity
first unsupported transmission step
horizon of the claimed structural effect
```

Do not invent an undefined technology. Verify it or keep the supplied scenario explicitly hypothetical.

## 3. One-Evidence Update

When the request adds one new fact to an existing judgment, remain in quick mode unless a rebuild trigger is crossed. Record:

```text
dated prior judgment or baseline_reconstructed
new fact with publication, event, and effective dates
affected premise or causal edge
update relation: strengthen / weaken / condition / refine / falsify / no_change
observed change now
expected next change and horizon
what did not change
verification trigger or rebuild_required
```

If the prior artifact is unavailable, request it when practical or label the baseline `baseline_reconstructed`; never imply that a reconstructed thesis was previously held.

## 4. Measurement, Time, And Counterevidence Check

Apply this only to numbers or dates that could change the decision. Do not turn Quick Scan into a full data audit.

- Separate publication, data-period, event/effective, observation, and access dates. A new report may describe an old cohort.
- State the geography, population, denominator, sample inclusion, and completion status behind a decisive number. Do not replace an industry denominator with a local case.
- Keep unlike units and states separate: capacity is not delivered output; application, approval, construction, and operation are not interchangeable; component price is not project capex or lifecycle cost.
- Check survivorship and selection: completed cases do not automatically estimate the current applicant population, and failed or withdrawn cases cannot silently disappear.
- Do not splice incompatible years, subsidies, currencies, definitions, or samples into one trend. If source versions conflict, record version/page and leave a decision-relevant difference unresolved rather than selecting a convenient number.

Retain one output line: `关键口径限制 / Measurement limit: ...`. Keep a decisive limitation in the main answer.

Run at least one dedicated search for a counterexample or competing explanation when search is available. Record the search question, strongest contrary evidence, source independence, and whether it changed the boundary or judgment. A failed search is not successful falsification. Keep a stipulated shock `[H]` intact as a scenario while separately testing whether its real-world premises are `[F]`.

## 5. Research Stop Rule

Prefer primary or first-party sources. Use enough independent evidence to cover the main chain, strongest alternative, current activation, and any claimed owner leverage; typically this is a small source set rather than an exhaustive archive. Stop when further accessible evidence is unlikely to change the bounded decision across structure, activation, capture, and the strongest alternative, or when the stated quick-scan effort boundary is reached. Then return `unresolved/unknown` and name the decisive missing fact rather than continuing indefinitely.

Continue to a full audit only when the quick scan finds a material unresolved dependency such as:

- company capture depends on contracts, qualification, or segment economics that are needed for the user's decision;
- zooming in may reverse the parent conclusion and that reversal matters to the decision;
- current activation cannot be separated from structural potential with the quick evidence boundary;
- a specific unresolved technology-transmission or rights question would change the requested decision and requires full evidence;
- evidence disagreement affects a material decision;
- a versioned report, dataset, visualization, or formal prediction is required.

Do not silently upgrade. Finish the quick scan and name the specific unresolved dependency whose resolution would change the decision. If the user requested full at the outset, enter full directly; this upgrade rule applies only to work that began quick.

## 6. One-Page Output

Use compact prose and a small table when useful. For an ordinary Chinese Quick Scan, aim for roughly 500-800 Chinese characters of main text, not as a mechanical gate. Move repeated definitions, full source metadata, and secondary judgments to a short appendix; never move the decisive unknown, measurement limit, or strongest objection out of the main answer merely to meet the target.

### Decision Card

- **Question:** literal decision question.
- **Memory sentence:** one bounded takeaway.
- **Depth/status:** `quick_scan`; evidence cutoff date.
- **Case boundary:** plain-language `Y/Q/Omega/t`.
- **Claim ceiling:** current supportable claim.
- **Non-goals:** investment recommendation, exhaustive market sizing, or other case-specific exclusions.

### Control Picture

Explain in ordinary language:

```text
who needs what
-> who or what must make it acceptable
-> who waits
-> what switching forces the system to redo
-> who may retain money or decision power
```

### Judgment Table

| Question | Quick judgment |
|---|---|
| Structural class | cone / cluster / cyclical / network / unresolved |
| Candidate bottleneck | function, not broad sector label |
| Current activation | active / latent / easing / unknown |
| Strongest bypass | substitute or counterforce |
| Successor constraint | where released flow likely goes next, or unknown/none identified |
| System power | why the node matters to accepted output |
| Company-node mapping | strong / partial / coalition / no clean match / unknown |
| Retained leverage | supported / conditional / unsupported / unknown; name the lever and strongest counterpower |
| Evidence confidence | high / medium / low with the decisive missing fact |

### Evidence And Exit

- List the few decisive `[F]` facts, then `[I]` mechanism and `[U]` unknowns.
- State `关键口径限制 / Measurement limit` for any decisive statistic.
- Record the counterevidence search question, strongest surviving objection, source independence, and whether it changed the judgment.
- Give one structural falsifier and, when retained leverage is claimed, one separate capture-directed falsifier.
- Give one review trigger.
- End with `stop at quick` or `upgrade to full because ...`.

## 7. Claim Discipline

- Do not use internal scores in the memory sentence.
- Do not name a winner before locating the functional constraint.
- Do not turn availability into adoption, adoption into structural change, or structure into owner rent.
- Company-node mapping does not establish retained leverage. A capture claim needs an observable price, allocation, contract, data, validation, or decision-control lever plus its strongest counterpower.
- Do not present a local case as an industry denominator.
- Do not hide a decisive unknown in an appendix.
- If evidence access fails, return a scoped `tool_blocked` card with the unanswered questions.
